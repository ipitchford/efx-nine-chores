## Checked reductions and historical search bounds

The self-contained argument is in [zero_minimum_reduction.md](zero_minimum_reduction.md), with independent review in [zero_minimum_independent_review.md](../verification/zero_minimum_independent_review.md).

1. Lower each row's selected global-minimum chore to zero, leaving all other entries unchanged. Every owned bundle's worst deletion residual is unchanged, while comparison-bundle costs can only decrease. Thus every EFX allocation after lowering was EFX before lowering, and every counterexample is preserved. Literal zero deletion is essential.
2. A counterexample has an integer representative with one zero per row and all other costs at most **3831**. The row-local active system has eight positive variables, and Hadamard's inequality gives the exact bound `floor(sqrt(7*8^7))`. This is 24 positive integer variables, 12-bit costs, and 15-bit full-row sums because each row has a fixed zero.
3. Raising the zero minima of that integer representative to one half and then multiplying by two gives a positive integer counterexample with common minimum **exactly one**, all other entries even and at most **7662**, and retained failed-comparison margins at least one.
4. Independently setting one positive off-minimum reference cost per row to one leaves **21 free real variables** for nine chores and 18 for eight. References `[1,0,0]` are valid under distinct pinned minima. This is the normalization used in the new strict real formulas; these formulas impose no integer bound or uniform margin.

The zero-minimum lemma and integer bound do not need distinct minimum chores. Restricting the emitted formulas to three distinct pinned minima additionally uses the previously documented shared-minimum insertion reduction and the eight-chore existence baseline (seven chores for the eight-chore calibration). The complete literal allocation clauses then cover the canonical residual. No row-total comparison is imposed.
